# Code of Conduct
We, as a team, try to enrich the world of coding without any prejudiced opinion or the intent to manipulate thoughts or perspectives. <br>
Our team has absolutely no political or educational intention. <br>
It’s our duty to support the idea of free speech, free information and free software accessible by the public, no matter how wealthy, what nationality, religion, physical or personality traits the person might have or be.<br>
We do not censor, we do not judge, we want to help people solve their coding problems, to create high quality software in ever shorter time.<br>
It’s our strong believe, that open source software enhances the world's knowledge pool and offers the safest and most transparent code. Open source is the idea of a fair, unprejudiced management of talents and ideas. Let the best people win, not the wealthiest.<br> 
Apart from that, we are certain, that the great majority of GitHub contributors use an appropriate language and value any type of privacy. Many work as professional developers and know from their working place, that harassing language or stealing information will not be tolerated.<br> 
We don't want to establish strict rules for just a few people, disregarding respect and common sense.  
As a first guide: We trust in your competence and intelligence, unless someone misuses that goodwill.
